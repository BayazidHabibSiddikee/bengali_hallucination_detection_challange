### How to skip the 3-hour wait on future runs:

1. When your job finishes, download  banglabert_large.pt  from the Kaggle Output.
2. Go to Kaggle, click Create -> New Dataset, and upload that  .pt  file (name it something like Trained BanglaBERT Hallucination).
3. The next time you want to test a new idea (like tweaking the LLM settings or ensemble weights), just add that dataset to your notebook.
4. You can then comment out the 3-hour  train_backbone()  code in Cell 10, and replace it with:
m.load_state_dict(torch.load("/kaggle/input/your-dataset-name/banglabert_large.pt"))
